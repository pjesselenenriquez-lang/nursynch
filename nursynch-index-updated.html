About NURSYNCH

NURSYNCH (Nursing Services Synchronization System) is a proposed web-based daily logbook for recording infirmary service counts by date and shift, with a format intended to support nursing documentation and administrative review.

This is an academic prototype, not an official MSU system. The text-based university heading is a placeholder and does not reproduce an official seal or wordmark.
